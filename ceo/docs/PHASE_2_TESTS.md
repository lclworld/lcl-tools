# Phase 2 checks: Inbox and Orders

Tick each one on your phone after the Hub is updated.

## Inbox
- [ ] Inbox opens and shows Unread by category.
- [ ] A new inquiry lands in New Inquiries with a one line summary.
- [ ] A supplier email lands in Suppliers, a staff email in Team.
- [ ] Gmail shows the same labels under LCL/.
- [ ] Moving an email to another category sticks, and the next email from that sender lands there too.
- [ ] A label submission has a Check this label button that opens the Label Compliance Checker.
- [ ] A reply written in the app arrives in the sender's inbox, in the same thread, only after you tap Send.
- [ ] Open in Gmail opens the right email.

## Orders
- [ ] Paid Zoho invoices from the last 120 days appear as orders.
- [ ] A new paid order shows on Home as "New paid order" with Confirm.
- [ ] Each status shows the email first. Test with your own email address in To: nothing goes until you tap Send.
- [ ] Pickup and delivery ask for the date, and the date appears in the email.
- [ ] Save the status without emailing works and is marked that way in the history.
- [ ] An order with no update for 5 working days shows on Home.
- [ ] Once INSTABRAND_SUBJECT is set, InstaBrand order emails become order cards.

# Phase 1 checks

Tick each one on your phone at lclworld.github.io/lcl-tools/ceo/

## Sign in
- [ ] Google sign in works with your account.
- [ ] Another Google account is refused.
- [ ] The PIN screen shows once a day, not every time you open the app.
- [ ] A wrong PIN says how many tries are left. Five wrong locks the phone for 15 minutes and emails you.
- [ ] Each unlock sends you a login alert email.
- [ ] Add to Home Screen works and opens without the browser bar.

## Home
- [ ] Needs you shows at most 5 items, with See all when there are more.
- [ ] Approve on a Manager Attention item marks it Approved in Operations Centre.
- [ ] Approve on a purchase changes it to Approved in Operations Centre.
- [ ] Undo puts each one back.
- [ ] Today lists today's production, deliveries, calendar events and your tasks, and hides when there are none.
- [ ] Search finds a client, a product code and an ingredient.

## Work
- [ ] Your decisions, Operations and Stock each open from one button.
- [ ] Changing a production status shows in Operations Centre after it syncs.
- [ ] Moving a production off WAITING FOR DIRECTOR closes its Manager Attention item, like Operations Centre does.
- [ ] A new task appears in Operations Centre for the person you picked.
- [ ] Removing an item from the low stock list shows the same way in Inventory.
- [ ] Pushed batches show the same on every phone.

## Toolbox
- [ ] Each of the four tools opens inside the app, and Back returns to the list.

## Offline
- [ ] With flight mode on, the app still opens and shows the last data.
- [ ] A change made offline is sent once you are back online.
